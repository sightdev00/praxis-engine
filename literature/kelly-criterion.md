# Kelly Criterion

This file stores source links only. Kelly must be distilled independently before it is combined with Bayesian belief updating or the Mao-derived practical-reasoning system.

## Primary source

- J. L. Kelly Jr. (1956), *A New Interpretation of Information Rate*, Bell System Technical Journal 35(4), 917-926 — DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1956.tb03809.x
- Bell Labs publication page: https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/publications-and-media/publications/a-new-interpretation-of-information-rate/

## Major later treatment

- Leonard C. MacLean, Edward O. Thorp & William T. Ziemba (eds.) (2011), *The Kelly Capital Growth Investment Criterion: Theory and Practice* — publisher/metadata: https://ideas.repec.org/b/wsi/wsbook/7598.html
- SSRN record: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1797366

## Important critical/counterpoint literature
