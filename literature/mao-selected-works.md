# Selected Works of Mao Tse-tung — Volumes I-IV

## Canonical online reading set

For Phase 1, use the second-edition online text hosted by 求是网 as the primary web reading set. The site notes that the second edition preserves the original selection while correcting dates, factual annotations, typographical issues, and adding `反对本本主义`.

- Volume I: https://www.qstheory.cn/zl/llzz/mzdxjd1j/
- Volume II: https://www.qstheory.cn/zl/llzz/mzdxjd2j/
- Volume III: https://www.qstheory.cn/zl/llzz/mzdxjd3j/
- Volume IV: https://www.qstheory.cn/zl/llzz/mzdxjd4j/
- Theory-books index: https://www.qstheory.cn/zl/llzz/

## Parallel searchable archive

Use this as a secondary searchable archive and for locating variant/earlier texts. Do **not** silently substitute a variant text for the second-edition text used by the main distillation pipeline.

- Chinese Marxists Internet Archive — Mao index: https://www.marxists.org/chinese/maozedong/
- Extended collected works / supplementary volumes index: https://www.marxists.org/chinese/maozedong/collect/index.htm

## Edition rule

Every article card must record the exact source URL and, when a materially different earlier/unedited text is consulted, record that variant separately.

Do not infer a general principle from an editorial difference until the difference itself has been checked.

## Phase-1 scope

Only Volumes I-IV belong to the initial Mao distillation. Later writings, recollections, secondary biographies, and commentary can be added as contextual sources, but they must not alter the Phase-1 corpus without an explicit scope decision.
