# Compounding and Multiplicative Growth

This file stores source links only. “Compounding” in Praxis Engine must eventually be treated more broadly than a savings-account formula: the relevant object is repeated multiplicative growth under uncertainty, including drawdowns and path dependence.

## Baseline mathematical reference

- U.S. SEC Investor.gov — Compound Interest: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/compound-interest
- U.S. SEC Investor.gov — Compound Interest Calculator: https://www.investor.gov/financial-tools-calculators/calculators/compound-interest-calculator

## Growth-optimal / multiplicative-growth literature

- J. L. Kelly Jr. (1956), *A New Interpretation of Information Rate*: https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1956.tb03809.x
- MacLean, Thorp & Ziemba (eds.) (2011), *The Kelly Capital Growth Investment Criterion: Theory and Practice*: https://ideas.repec.org/b/wsi/wsbook/7598.html
- MacLean, Thorp & Ziemba (2010), *Long-Term Capital Growth: The Good and Bad Properties of the Kelly and Fractional Kelly Capital Growth Criteria*: https://doi.org/10.1080/14697688.2010.504474

## Counterpoint to retain

- Paul A. Samuelson (1979), *Why We Should Not Make Mean Log of Wealth Big Though Years to Act Are Long* — retain as a challenge to any attempt to universalize log-growth maximization.
