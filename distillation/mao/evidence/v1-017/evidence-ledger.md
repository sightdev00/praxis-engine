## Variant-sensitive observations

The earlier lecture-outline text is especially important because `实践论` was not simply a timeless standalone essay in origin; it existed inside a broader philosophical lecture framework. The earlier source includes:

- explicit discussion of direct vs indirect experience;
- the sensory -> rational transformation;
- war and work examples;
- return of rational knowledge to practice;
- correction of incomplete/incorrect theory through practice.

The Selected Works text should therefore be treated as an edited canonical version, not automatically as a verbatim transcript of the original lecture.

## Adversarial evidence questions

1. Does practical success discriminate the proposed theory from competing explanations?
2. How does the method distinguish execution failure from model failure?
3. What prevents repeated practice from reinforcing local bias rather than correcting it?
4. What makes the evidence `rich enough` and `reality-conforming` before abstraction?
5. When practical testing is irreversible or destructive, what substitutes or safeguards are permitted?
6. How should indirect evidence be weighted against direct experience when the latter is small or biased?

## Evidence-quality conclusion

Textual confidence that Mao endorsed a recursive practice-knowledge-practice model is **high**.

Empirical confidence in the broad idea that action/observation can correct models is **high**, but the stronger universal claim that each cycle reliably reaches a higher level is **low-to-medium without additional controls**.

Praxis Engine should therefore preserve the recursive architecture while adding explicit controls for hypothesis competition, evidence quality, reversibility, error localization and independent validation.
