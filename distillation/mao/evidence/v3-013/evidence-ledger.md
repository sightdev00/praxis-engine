## Evidence quality summary

- Strongest mechanism: iterative pilot-to-scale and opinion-action-feedback loops can correct abstract directives and expose implementation detail.
- Strongest governance insight: unified responsibility and priority limits address fragmented authority and overload.
- Strongest counterexample: the named application to cadre examination was followed by a mass misclassification campaign and coercive errors.
- Main causal gap: 1942 “successful” units and “unsuccessful” units are asserted without common metrics or alternative explanations.
- Main epistemic gap: leaders choose samples, synthesize opinions, define correct direction and judge results.
- Main rights gap: high-consequence classification lacks defense, independent review, evidence threshold and reversible action in the June text.

## Variant audit

The QST body and public Chinese comparison preserve the same nine numbered sections and two foreign-reference notes. Historical sources identify the original title as `关于领导方法的决定`; the Selected Works title is broader, but no material body variant was identified in the accessible comparison.

The critical historical variant is procedural rather than textual: corrective rules added from late July through October 1943 are not contained in the June decision. They must remain a later revision layer.

## Adversarial checkpoints

1. How are pilot units selected, and where are failure/random samples?
2. Does leader presence distort reports or behavior?
3. Is the original opinion distribution preserved before synthesis?
4. Can people reject the returned “systematic opinion” without penalty?
5. What outcome can falsify the leadership's synthesis?
6. Are active/middle/backward labels independent of agreement with the core?
7. Who can replace or review the core and chief leader?
8. Which tasks require professional independence rather than mass participation?
9. What higher safeguards apply to accusation, detention and punishment?
10. Which basic functions and rights remain protected from the single center task?
