## Evidence quality summary

- Strongest source contribution: explicit state change, carrying-capacity mismatch, adversary pressure and sunk-cost resistance.
- Strongest later evidence: multi-round implementation, measurable personnel/ratio reductions, frontline/local reinforcement and differentiated treatment of removed personnel.
- Weakest claims: exactly two years to victory; monotonic worsening of material conditions; uniform applicability; automatic conversion from smaller size to greater capability.
- Main validation gap: region-level before/after mission output, total burden including unpaid work, service degradation, personnel harm, minimum redundancy and counterfactual comparison.

## Variant audit

The QST second-edition text and public Chinese comparison have the same four-paragraph argument, publication role and main examples. The most material provenance addition comes from the Selected Works note: the proposal originated with Li Dingming and other Border Region councillors before central adoption.

This delivery metadata matters. The body presents “the Central Committee proposed” as the immediate policy authority; the note preserves an external coalition input. Both can be true only if proposal origin and adoption/scaling authority remain separate fields.

## Adversarial checkpoints

1. What are the denominator and thresholds for “fish” and “water” in each region?
2. Which capabilities are critical, redundant, low-value or merely politically disfavored?
3. How were removed personnel compensated, protected and re-employed?
4. Did lower headcount reduce total social burden or shift it to unpaid civilians/local units?
5. Which material indicators worsened after 1942, which improved, and due to what interventions?
6. Was “two years” a target, forecast, mobilizing slogan or planning horizon?
7. What stop-loss and re-expansion conditions prevent over-cutting?
8. How much of later resilience is attributable to resizing rather than production, finance, trade, international war and enemy changes?
9. How did the original Li Dingming proposal change during central adoption and local scaling?
