## Evidence quality summary

- Strongest evidence: historical existence of multiple operating-mode transitions and explicit recognition of two opposite transition errors.
- Weaker evidence: single-cause explanations of historical defeats and “correct guidance” claims.
- Forward-looking evidence: future regularization claims must remain predictions, not retrospective facts.

## Adversarial checkpoints

1. 后续是否出现正规化/集中化过度导致新的损失？
2. 哪些 capability 是真正不可替代，哪些只是当时环境下被高估？
3. “核心能力产生邻接资产”是否有其它领域独立证据？
4. 未来正规化预测的具体条件、时间和实际结果如何匹配？
