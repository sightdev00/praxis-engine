## Evidence-quality assessment

Strengths:
- operational/legal document rather than retrospective essay;
- explicit protected state, admission gate and enforcement sequence;
- shows coalition strategy translated into local administration.

Weaknesses:
- alleged hostile actions are not independently audited;
- authority defines both protected state and prohibited disruption;
- no procedural detail sufficient for modern coercive-action standards.

## Cross-volume relevance

- V2-004/V2-006: coalition + independent capability;
- V1-012: cooperation does not erase core capability;
- V1-004 revision cases: silent institutional changes can remove necessary functions;
- modern mapping: change-control / migration invariants / authorization boundaries.

## Modern transfer boundary

Transfer only structural controls:

```text
protected state
explicit change authority
admission/authentication
proportionate validation
```

Do **not** transfer the historical punitive/security practice without modern legal, rights and review safeguards.
