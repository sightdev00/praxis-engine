## Evidence-quality assessment

Strength:
- highly concrete behavior taxonomy;
- differentiates hidden disagreement, gossip, personal conflict, weak planning and non-correction;
- connects individual/local incentives with collective execution.

Weakness:
- almost entirely `O6`;
- no sampling, incidence, comparative control or independent outcome measurement;
- root-cause attribution is ideologically overdetermined;
- power asymmetry and retaliation risk are missing variables;
- article does not specify how to distinguish principled dissent from deviance under incorrect leadership.

## Adversarial questions

1. What if the formal organization/leader is wrong?
2. What safe channel exists for dissent after commitment?
3. Can back-channel criticism be a symptom of unsafe formal channels rather than individual selfishness?
4. Which of the eleven behaviors are causes vs symptoms of organizational dysfunction?
5. How much disagreement improves decision quality before coordination cost dominates?
