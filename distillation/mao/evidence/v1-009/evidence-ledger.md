## What the document can establish

Strongly:
- what relations and variables the classifier uses;
- that single land-ownership status is insufficient;
- that frequency/dominance of income behavior matters;
- that the standard was connected to consequential policy execution.

It cannot establish by itself:
- reliability across regions and time;
- inter-rater agreement among classifiers;
- misclassification rates;
- causal completeness for political behavior;
- fairness or total outcome quality of the policy using the classifier.

## High-risk inference boundary

Because policy consequences are substantial, any modern methodological transfer should focus on **classifier design and audit**, not on transplanting the historical categories themselves.
