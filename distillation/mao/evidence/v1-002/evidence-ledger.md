## Evidence-quality audit

### Strengths

- direct field contact is explicit;
- multiple counties are compared;
- group, stage and before/after comparisons are used;
- quantitative estimates appear alongside qualitative reports;
- unresolved issues and policy side effects are not completely suppressed.

### Weaknesses

1. **Sampling bias** — five counties were important movement sites, not a representative probability sample.
2. **Informant bias** — main informants were peasants and movement workers, partly correcting gentry bias but introducing another standpoint.
3. **Participant-observer bias** — author is also a political advocate and strategist.
4. **Causal confounding** — North Expedition, regime change and military developments co-occur with local organization.
5. **Short observation window** — many long-run effects are unavailable during 32 days.
6. **Measurement quality** — membership/population numbers are estimates and administrative reports, not modern validated datasets.
7. **Normative-causal mixing** — some conclusions about what is necessary or correct follow political objectives as well as observations.

## Current-protocol reconstruction

The strongest methodological contribution is **not** simply `go to the field`.

A more precise reconstruction is:

```text
conflicting high-stakes narratives
-> move closer to decision-relevant processes
-> collect multiple evidence types
-> segment by actor/location/stage
-> compare before/after and across cases
-> decompose evaluative labels into observable mechanisms
-> record unresolved effects and policy interactions
-> revise action where feedback exposes side effects
```

But this still lacks explicit competing-hypothesis and causal-identification controls.

## Legacy-card corrections

### `Investigation priority`

Retain, but narrow to:

> When decision-relevant reality is directly observable and existing accounts are distant/conflicted, obtain closer evidence before committing.

Do not translate to `direct observation always dominates indirect evidence`.

### `Enabling constraint`

Retain only as a topology hypothesis. V1-003 later disproves its universality as a mandatory single-bottleneck rule.

### `Organization is a capability multiplier`

Supported later across military and organization texts, but avoid the generic `multiplier` metaphor when actual mechanism is unknown.

### `Second-order effects`

Strong modern mapping of multiple policy examples, but not source terminology. Later economic/governance texts must test whether this becomes a systematic habit.

### `Do not substitute for participant agency`

Keep at `P1/P2-test` rather than universalizing; revolutionary leadership context contains both agency-building and coercive practices.

## Re-audit result

V1-002 remains a major operational source for Praxis Engine because it includes method, data, comparative structure, side effects and unresolved cases. The current protocol mainly changes **how confidently and how generically** we interpret those materials.

No Volume-I synthesis should cite the old article card's modern abstractions without the boundaries in this ledger.
