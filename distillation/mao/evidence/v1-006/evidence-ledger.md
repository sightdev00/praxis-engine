## Strongest method evidence

- `E05`: local-to-global generalization explicitly criticized;
- `E09`: author explicitly marks his own earlier argument as insufficient;
- `E11`: rule applicability conditioned on context rather than slogan;
- `E12-E13`: timing forecast explicitly corrected and separated from directional forecast.

## Main empirical caution

The central predictive thesis — that a revolutionary high tide would arrive relatively quickly — is defended through a broad structural theory and many selected indicators. The text itself is valuable evidence of the **forecasting method**, but not sufficient evidence that the forecast was well calibrated.
