## Evidence judgment

### Valuable mechanism

```text
observable costly support
>
cheap self-description
```

### Main inference error risk

```text
specific past support
-> actor-class universalization
-> permanent identity
```

The first arrow may be evidentially useful; the second and third require additional counterexample search and revalidation.

## Adversarial checkpoints

1. 同期或后期其它国家/主体是否提供过可比的实质援助？
2. 苏联政策与中国利益后来是否出现冲突？
3. 如果出现冲突，源文本的“朋友”模型是否被修订？
4. 最终 Skill 应把 `current alignment`, `trust`, `dependency`, `permanent identity` 分成不同字段。
