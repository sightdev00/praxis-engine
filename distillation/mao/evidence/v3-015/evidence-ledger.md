## Evidence quality summary

- Strongest design evidence: implementation checks, local pilots, skill training, voluntary/equivalent labor, compensation/apology and annual review are concrete actions.
- Strongest contextual support: rent reduction and labor mutual aid were implemented across several bases and are associated in later histories with production and mobilization gains.
- Main causal gap: policies were deployed as a bundle under changing military, weather and resource conditions; no common regional counterfactual isolates each actuator.
- Main coercion gap: universal participation and moralized “good citizen/leader” standards sit uneasily with voluntariness; later summaries rarely report refusal, unequal burden or failed groups.
- Main repair gap: self-criticism by each side is not independent adjudication of the other side's harms.

## Variant audit

The QST and Marxists comparison texts preserve the same three-section structure, dates, major numerical heuristics, voluntary/equivalent condition and annual civil-military review. No material body variant was identified in the accessible versions.

The document must be read with the 1942 land-policy boundary: rent and interest reduction was paired with post-reduction payment and protection of anti-Japanese landlords' rights. Omitting that upstream policy would turn a coalition-constrained reform into a different policy.

## Adversarial checkpoints

1. What exact rent baseline and evidence determine “thorough” implementation?
2. Can tenants, landlords and labor-group members appeal without retaliation?
3. How are voluntary participation and exit measured under cadre mobilization?
4. Are labor exchanges equivalent after valuing skill, livestock, timing and risk?
5. What is net output after tools, land, supervision, injury and mission opportunity cost?
6. Which locality constraints make a model transferable or non-transferable?
7. Does the 90/10 heuristic improve household surplus and fair revenue, not just reported activity?
8. Can residents directly report army harms rather than wait for army self-criticism?
9. Are compensation and apology completed, and does recurrence fall next year?
10. Which tasks, people and minimum rest/rights are exempt from universal mobilization?
