## Cross-check against operational corpus

### Supports

- V1-013: general vs special laws; specific war requires specific strategic method;
- V1-015: one relation can become politically principal while others remain;
- V1-012/V1-016: actor relations and strategy change as external contradiction structure changes;
- V1-003/V1-004: conditions and force structure are dynamic rather than static.

### Counterpressure

- V1-003: survival depends on a configuration of multiple conditions, including some necessary-like conditions;
- V1-008/V1-010: central task coexists with supporting subsystems that cannot simply be ignored;
- V1-013: counteroffensive readiness can depend on several conditions, not one unique bottleneck;
- revision ledger: interventions can create new constraints and opposition, so solving a principal issue can expose or generate secondary blockers.

## Adversarial evidence questions

1. Is `one principal contradiction` descriptively true in every complex system, or only a useful prioritization heuristic in some systems?
2. How is the principal contradiction measured rather than retrospectively named?
3. Can multiple jointly necessary constraints each block success even if one relation has greater strategic weight?
4. Can stochastic/environmental systems change without a useful binary-opposition representation?
5. Does the dominant side determine system character, or is it merely correlated with it?
6. What prevents analysts from labeling the issue they already prefer as the `principal` one?
7. How should principal-relation analysis coexist with risk constraints that are secondary in causal importance but catastrophic if violated?

## Variant audit priorities

A full later audit should compare at least:

- wording around `差异/矛盾`;
- formulation of `主要矛盾` and `一切问题迎刃而解`;
- examples used to justify universal contradiction;
- language around principal aspect and exceptional dominance of theory/superstructure;
- antagonism section.

## Evidence-quality conclusion

Textual confidence is **high** for the following cluster:

```text
specificity
+ relational analysis
+ stage differentiation
+ unequal strategic importance
+ dynamic dominance
+ conditional transformation
+ antagonism as only one form
```

Empirical confidence varies sharply. The broad methodological injunction to preserve specificity and avoid uniform treatment is strong; the universal ontological and `one principal contradiction in every complex process` claims require substantial narrowing before modern transfer.
