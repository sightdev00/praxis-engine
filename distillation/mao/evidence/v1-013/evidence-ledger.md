## Evidence architecture

### Strongest evidence for Praxis methods

- `E01-E03`: general knowledge must be re-specialized by domain/context;
- `E04-E05`: global relevance is relational and context-dependent;
- `E06-E07`: explicit evidence -> judgment -> plan -> action -> re-observation -> revision loop;
- `E08`: inherited rules are tested, retained/rejected/extended;
- `E15-E17`: condition configuration and local/global linkage;
- `E18-E20`: resource concentration is localized and qualified, not absolute;
- `E21-E24`: planning horizon and method change with stage and environment.

### Major evidence caution

The work is a 1936 retrospective synthesis after major internal political/military disputes. It is very strong evidence for **Mao's mature reconstruction of his strategic method**, but weaker as independent evidence that every earlier defeat had exactly the cause he assigns or that every proposed counterfactual would have succeeded.

### Transfer boundary

The military methods involving destruction, coercion, deception or armed conflict must remain historical. Modern transfer is restricted to abstract reasoning/control structures such as context-sensitive transfer, evidence synthesis, iterative replanning, resource concentration, stage adaptation and failure analysis.
