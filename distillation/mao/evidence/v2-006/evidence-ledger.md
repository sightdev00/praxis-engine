## Evidence-quality assessment

Strengths:
- real adverse outcomes force renewed assessment;
- current state is represented with both negative and positive variables;
- multiple scenario branches and qualitative likelihood comparison;
- explicit time/threshold prediction;
- concrete organizational rollbacks/corrections;
- later note provides a clear failed expectation.

Weaknesses:
- political factional framing strongly shapes causal attribution;
- many factor weights are qualitative;
- later editorial notes favor one retrospective interpretation;
- the new-army forecast lacks transparent derivation;
- `principal danger` can be retrospectively selected by leadership narrative.

## Prediction audit targets

- PRED-006-A: KMT reform is possible.
- PRED-006-B: 250k-300k new-type troops in 6-12 months -> battlefield turning signs.
- PRED-006-C: anti-surrender forces will dominate nationally.

## Revision relevance

- restoration of political commissar / political department after coalition-induced changes;
- correction of accommodation in Xi'an/Longdong;
- later failure of expected KMT reform.

These should be connected to `revision-ledger.md` and `prediction-ledger.md`.
