## Evidence conclusion

本文提供了一个有价值但非控制实验的对照：相同名义任务下，不同工作方法与不同参与结果共同出现。最可靠的抽象不是“关心生活必定产生支持”，而是：

```text
when participation is required,
participant conditions and implementation method are causal candidates that must be observed rather than treated as external details.
```
