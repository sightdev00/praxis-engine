## Current-protocol reconstruction

### What survives strongly

1. **The article begins from a decision problem**, not from taxonomy for its own sake.
2. **Broad groups are internally heterogeneous** and are segmented by material position/trajectory.
3. **Current stance is not necessarily fixed**; the article predicts movement under changed political conditions.
4. **Capability is not inferred from headcount alone**; concentration/structural position enters the worker analysis.

### What must be narrowed from the legacy card

The original card translated the source too quickly into:

```text
actor / stakes / incentives / capabilities
```

That mapping remains useful only at the `I/A` layer. The source's own model is much more specific:

```text
economic/class position
-> material interest / pressure
-> expected revolutionary attitude
-> alliance/opposition strategy
```

Modern transfer must not erase this theoretical ontology.

## Adversarial reading

1. The source relies heavily on class-economic inference rather than systematic direct observation.
2. Political behavior can be affected by identity, institutions, culture, local history, information and organization beyond economic position; V1-004 already demonstrates such complications.
3. Predicted group behavior may be partly theory-confirming because categories and expected attitudes are constructed together.
4. Historical strike examples are selected behavioral evidence, not a representative sample.
5. Strong political categories can make exceptions harder to notice.

## Re-audit result

The original six article-local `G1` items should be understood as follows under the new ladder:

- internal segmentation: `P2 candidate` only because later articles independently reinforce it;
- stage-dependent movement: `P2 candidate` after later united-front and military texts;
- structural capability beyond raw number: `P2 candidate` after later organization/military texts;
- generic `stakes not labels` formulation: retain as a modern mapping, **not** as a direct source principle;
- unstable allies / differentiated treatment: later evidence supports tracking reliability, but this article alone is insufficient.

No Volume-I conclusion should cite the legacy card without this audit note.
