## Variant audit note

The selected-works version can be read as:

```text
local / temporary facts
-> mistaken whole-picture judgment
```

The earlier variant appears closer to:

```text
high-level estimate
+ selected local facts interpreted through it
-> mistaken whole-picture judgment
```

These are methodologically different failure modes. The first emphasizes local-vs-global weighting; the second emphasizes model-conditioned evidence interpretation. Therefore no general mechanism should be extracted from this passage without preserving the version distinction.

## Evidence-quality assessment

Strengths:

- the article clearly records internal competing judgments rather than presenting only the final line;
- it separates stage attainment from stage consolidation;
- it distinguishes long-term objectives from immediate-stage work;
- it treats implementation among the masses as a separate execution problem.

Weaknesses:

- most core evidence is `O6` strategic inference rather than independent measurement;
- the article is a meeting conclusion written to resolve internal disagreement, which may encourage ex-post coherence;
- variant changes materially affect the apparent reasoning model;
- many causal weights are asserted qualitatively.

## Cross-article relevance

Potential support for existing patterns:

- `P2`: actor maps and tactics must be re-estimated when the external stage changes;
- `P2`: a stage priority can rise without eliminating subordinate tensions;
- `P2`: strategic judgment must be converted into organization/execution rather than treated as self-executing;
- `P2`: immediate tasks and long-horizon objectives require explicit separation.

Do not upgrade any of these solely from this article.
