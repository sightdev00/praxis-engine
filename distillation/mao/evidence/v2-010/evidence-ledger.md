## Evidence quality summary

- Strongest value: **textual evidence for the author's organizational control model**.
- Weaker value: independent proof that every claimed mechanism caused success/failure.
- Primary risk: retrospective attribution and political classification are mixed with causal analysis.

## Adversarial checkpoints

1. 后续“报告制度/党委制/领导方法”是否真正提供上行坏消息机制？
2. 有哪些例子显示“干部能力强”却因战略错误而导致更大损失？
3. 开放扩张与安全筛选是否出现过误杀/漏检后果？
4. 民主监督在高纪律结构中是否有可观察的纠错案例？
