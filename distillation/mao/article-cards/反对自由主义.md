# Pass 0 — Historical frame

This text appears as the CCP moves into a large anti-Japanese united-front period and faces a larger, more heterogeneous organizational environment.

Unlike V2-001/V2-002, it is not primarily about external strategy. It asks how internal relations, personal incentives and communication habits can degrade collective execution.

The source is strongly normative and ideological. We should distinguish:

```text
observed behavioral anti-patterns
from
claimed root cause in class ideology
from
prescribed moral ideal
```

# Pass 1 — Source / argument reconstruction

## 1.1 Productive disagreement is separated from unprincipled peace

The opening claim is that active ideological struggle can produce unity useful for action, whereas suppressing disagreement for interpersonal peace creates organizational decay.

This is a striking complement to V1-005:

```text
before commitment: surface and resolve relevant disagreement
rather than hide it
```

## 1.2 Eleven manifestations form a behavior taxonomy

The article does not remain at the label `自由主义`. It decomposes it into observable patterns including:

- avoiding principled disagreement with acquaintances;
- complaining privately rather than using formal channels;
- staying silent when a problem does not affect oneself;
- refusing commands when personal opinion differs;
- turning principled debate into personal attack;
- failing to challenge/report harmful claims;
- failing to investigate/engage with people;
- failing to stop harm to collective interests;
- working without plan/direction;
- relying on seniority while neglecting work/learning;
- recognizing one's own mistake but refusing to correct it.

This decomposition is methodologically stronger than the abstract moral label alone.

## 1.3 The article distinguishes principled conflict from personal conflict

One listed failure mode is **not** excessive disagreement but the wrong objective of disagreement:

```text
problem-focused dispute for better collective result
vs
personal attack / resentment / revenge
```

Thus `conflict` itself is not treated as good or bad; purpose and channel matter.

## 1.4 Informal/back-channel criticism is treated as a coordination failure

The article contrasts:

```text
formal suggestion / meeting discussion
vs
private complaint / after-meeting talk
```

The implied mechanism is information routing: relevant disagreement that does not reach a decision-capable channel cannot correct collective action.

## 1.5 Local relationship incentives can override system-level objectives

Friendship, hometown ties, colleague/subordinate relationships and self-protection are described as reasons for not surfacing known problems.

This is significant because it shows a non-class-specific micro-mechanism inside organizations:

```text
local relational benefit / conflict avoidance
-> suppress corrective signal
-> collective policy remains uncorrected
```

## 1.6 Policy execution can fail despite formal agreement

The article's `说的是马克思主义，行的是自由主义` formulation distinguishes declared belief from operational behavior.

This is a continuation of Volume-I / V2-001 commitment-validation logic:

```text
stated doctrine
!=
behavioral implementation
```

## 1.7 The claimed root cause is ideological/class-based self-interest

The article attributes these behaviors to petty-bourgeois selfishness and prioritization of personal over revolutionary interests.

That causal diagnosis is much less empirically supported than the behavioral taxonomy itself.

# Pass 4 — Adversarial reading

1. `积极思想斗争` can become conformity pressure if authority determines in advance which view is `correct`.
2. The article gives no robust mechanism for protecting legitimate minority dissent from being labeled liberalism or opportunism.
3. The causal attribution to petty-bourgeois selfishness is ideological and underdetermined; similar behavior can arise from fear, power asymmetry, psychological safety problems, poor incentives, bad meeting design or retaliation risk.
4. `Report harmful speech` can become surveillance rather than healthy correction in organizations lacking rights/procedural safeguards.
5. Strict command obedience may conflict with V1-005's open deliberation if commitment boundaries are unclear or decisions are wrong.
6. The behavioral taxonomy is valuable descriptively, but a moralizing root-cause model can hide system-design causes.
7. There is no evidence here that more internal conflict always improves outcomes; conflict quality, channel and power conditions matter.

# First-pass takeaway

The transferable value is not `fight liberalism` as a moral slogan.

It is the organizational control insight:

```text
local incentives can suppress corrective signals;
hidden disagreement creates false unity;
correction needs an actionable and protected channel;
issue-focused challenge must be distinguished from personal conflict;
recognized error must change behavior to count as learning.
```

The article's ideological root-cause explanation and strong discipline assumptions require substantial modern narrowing.
