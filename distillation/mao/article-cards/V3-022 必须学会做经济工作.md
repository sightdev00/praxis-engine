# V3-022 必须学会做经济工作

> Status: first-pass complete under current protocol
> Date: 1945-01-10
> Document type: labour-model conference speech / wartime economic-capability policy

## A. Identification

- Primary source: *Mao Zedong Selected Works*, Vol. III, pp. 1014–1020; speech to the Shaan-Gan-Ning Border Region labour-hero and model-worker conference.

# Pass 0 — Historical frame

The immediate constraint is sustaining war, civilian life and reconstruction under uncertain external aid. The speech turns economic work from a specialist adjunct into a responsibility of cadres, military units and communities.

# Pass 1 — Source / argument reconstruction

```text
external aid may help but cannot be depended on
+ army-and-people creative effort
-> self-reliant production capacity

production + thrift + quality + mutual aid + exemplary diffusion
-> improved supply, morale and war endurance
```

The text calls for agriculture, industry and trade; production by units and institutions; saving labour/materials; better quantity and quality; and the diffusion of labour-hero experience.

# Pass 2 — Evidence audit

See `../evidence/v3-022/evidence-ledger.md`.

# Pass 3 — Reasoning reconstruction

1. treat external support as upside, not the sole plan;
2. connect production capacity to military and civilian resilience;
3. use local experimentation and exemplary workers to spread techniques;
4. constrain expansion by product quality and resource thrift.

# Pass 4 — Adversarial reading

Output gains may arise from weather, markets, coercion, unpaid labour, rationing or external transfers rather than models or contests. “Self-reliance” can conceal unequal burdens and prevent needed aid. Labour emulation needs hours, injury, compensation, distribution, consent and counterfactual productivity—not celebration alone.

# Pass 5 — Article-local candidate mechanisms

## H1-3-22-01 — Plan for external support without making it a dependency

Maintain an internal-capacity baseline while recording outside support, its reliability and the loss scenario.

- Textual confidence: high
- Empirical confidence: medium

## H1-3-22-02 — Scale exemplary practice through auditable experiments

Before diffusion, measure output, quality, labour time, safety, inputs and distribution; retain failure cases and prohibit compulsory imitation.

- Textual confidence: high
- Empirical confidence: medium

# Pass 6 — Cross-article update

V3-009 and V3-015 tied organizational load to production. This article adds quality, thrift and external-aid uncertainty, but requires V3-021's voluntary-participation boundary to prevent mobilization from becoming extraction.

## Working judgment

The transferable rule is resilience through measured local capacity, not autarkic rhetoric: external help may be welcomed, yet plans must survive its loss and account for human cost.
