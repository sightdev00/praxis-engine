# Pass 0 — Historical frame

The immediate problem is not simply whether the revolution will win. The document treats victory as vulnerable to two opposite policy failures:

```text
strategic fear of the enemy
-> surrender of initiative / right error

local contempt for the enemy or allies
-> reckless action / left error

either policy error left uncorrected
-> loss of support, production, order and ultimately victory
```

The decision pressure is a rapidly expanding revolutionary state that must carry out land reform, absorb or neutralize multiple social groups, establish representative government, and maintain production while still fighting. The intended audience is internal party and governmental leadership, not an open public audience. That delivery channel raises the importance of command clarity, implementation feedback and protection against local over-interpretation.

# Pass 1 — Source / argument reconstruction

## 1.1 Strategic confidence and local caution are made simultaneous

The document rejects an overall overestimate of imperialism and the Kuomintang while requiring serious respect for the enemy in every local military, political, economic and ideological struggle. Strategic confidence is therefore not a license for local carelessness.

```text
overall strategic confidence
+ local enemy assessment
+ concentrated effort
+ attention to allies
-> avoid both paralysis and reckless overreach
```

This is a scale-separation rule. It does not say that the enemy is weak in every encounter; it says that a weaker overall position can still produce strong local threats.

## 1.2 The left/right correction rule depends on current state

The appropriate corrective direction changes with conditions: after military victories, prevent left excess; after defeats or insufficient victories, prevent right retreat; before land-reform masses are mobilized, oppose right hesitation; after mobilization and struggle have begun, prevent left excess.

The article thus treats “left” and “right” as error directions relative to a state, not as fixed identities of particular people. It is a control rule, but its state variables are qualitative and its thresholds are not specified.

## 1.3 Poor-peasant leadership is coupled to a middle-peasant coalition

Poor and landless peasants are to initiate land reform and lead in peasant associations and village government. The document explicitly rejects the slogan that poor peasants alone “make and sit the country.” The intended coalition includes hired laborers, poor peasants, middle peasants and other laboring people; at national level it expands to workers, peasants including new rich peasants, independent business owners, harmed small and medium capitalists, students, educators, intellectuals, professionals, enlightened gentry, ordinary officials, oppressed minorities and overseas Chinese.

```text
poor-peasant initiative
-> reform is not captured by old rural power

middle-peasant inclusion
-> initiative becomes a governing coalition

broad coalition
-> durable national state capacity
```

The source's leadership model is therefore not identical to numerical domination by the poorest group.

## 1.4 Classification errors must be corrected and material losses returned

The document prohibits adventurous policy toward middle peasants. Misclassified people from the middle peasantry or other strata should be reclassified, and distributed property should be returned as far as possible. It uses a 25 percent threshold for distinguishing a middle peasant from a rich peasant when exploitative income is measured against total income; wealthy middle-peasant land may not be divided without consent.

This combines a classifier, a correction operation and a consent boundary. It does not specify who independently verifies the income denominator, how ambiguous cases appeal, or how quickly return must occur.

## 1.5 Property transformation is separated from useful production

The document rejects applying rural landlord/rich-peasant struggle mechanically to cities. It distinguishes the elimination of feudal exploitation from protection of useful commerce. Private industry and commerce that benefit the national economy are to be protected and encouraged. Landlords' and rich peasants' businesses are generally protected; only bureaucratic capital and the businesses of genuine local tyrants or counterrevolutionaries may be confiscated. Useful confiscated businesses must continue operating after state or people's reception rather than being dispersed or closed.

The source also proposes joint management committees in public and private enterprises to lower costs, increase production and balance public and private as well as labor and capital interests. Worker living conditions may improve, but excessive treatment is rejected. The operational boundary is production continuity, not a general guarantee of private property or worker bargaining equality.

## 1.6 Knowledge workers are treated as a mostly usable, minority-risk population

Students, teachers, professors, scientists, artists and ordinary intellectuals are described as mostly capable of joining the revolution or remaining neutral. Only a small minority are treated as firm counterrevolutionaries. The prescribed response is differentiated: unite, educate and employ according to circumstances; use an appropriate mass-line response against the small hard-core group.

This is a capacity-preservation rule with a political classifier attached. “Most” and “small minority” are not measured, and “firm counterrevolutionary” remains a high-consequence label.

## 1.7 Enlightened gentry are conditionally retained rather than uniformly removed

The document treats wartime cooperation with enlightened gentry in representative bodies and governments as necessary and successful. Those with political reliability and ability should remain in higher government; those without ability but with a good political record should have their livelihood maintained. Gentry-origin people whose property is subject to land reform may lose feudal land while being shielded from struggle when popular hostility is limited. People who infiltrated government, consistently harmed the people and are broadly hated are sent to people's courts as ordinary tyrant cases.

The source separates origin, current behavior, ability, public sentiment and property treatment. It still gives a political organization authority to decide which category applies, with no independent appeal procedure in the text.

## 1.8 New rich peasants, old rich peasants and changed class status are separated

New and old rich peasants are not treated as interchangeable. The document claims that encouraging new rich peasants and wealthy middle peasants during rent-and-interest reduction stabilized the middle peasantry and supported agricultural production. After land equalization, the priority is production, food, and mutual-aid or cooperative work. In old liberated areas, new rich peasants receive wealthy-middle-peasant treatment and their land cannot be divided without consent.

Landlords who have worked for more than five years, and rich peasants who have fallen to middle or poor status for more than three years, may have their class status changed if their conduct is good. Those retaining a large—not merely small—amount of surplus property must surrender the surplus according to the peasants' demands.

These are behavior-over-origin rules with time thresholds, but the text does not define “good conduct,” “large surplus,” or the evidence and review process for reclassification.

## 1.9 The center of land reform is land and productive property, not punitive treasure-hunting

The document defines the center of land reform as equalizing feudal land and related grain, livestock and tools, with rich peasants surrendering only surplus. It warns against making buried landlord property the main work or spending excessive time on it. Landlords and rich peasants must be treated differently under the Land Law Outline; large, medium and small landlords, and bully and non-bully landlords, should also be differentiated.

This is a scope and prioritization rule: preserve the main productive transformation and limit a side activity that can produce delay, arbitrary search and retaliatory escalation.

## 1.10 Coercion is bounded by “few killings,” review and transformation

The document permits execution of a very small number of genuinely egregious offenders after careful people's-court trial, government-committee approval and public announcement. It simultaneously insists on few killings and an absolute prohibition on random killing, arguing that excessive killing loses sympathy and isolates the party. It says the goal is to eliminate feudalism and landlords as a class, not eliminate every landlord as an individual, and to preserve and transform people who are not actively damaging the war or land reform.

For bad cadres, party members and harmful elements among workers and peasants, the preferred method is persuasion and properly conducted criticism and struggle, while preventing retaliation. Masses are said to have rights to criticize, remove or recommend removal, recommend expulsion, and send the worst cases to a people's court.

The text therefore combines coercive authority with anti-arbitrariness constraints. It does not establish an independent court, defense counsel, appeal, custody, compensation or an external audit of the “genuinely egregious” classifier.

## 1.11 Representative form and party leadership are stated together

The source defines the New Democratic state as a broad people's state under working-class leadership through the Communist Party. It states that people's congresses are the organs of power and that governments are elected by them. It describes village and district elections based on peasant assemblies or representative assemblies, and future elections at county, city, provincial and central levels.

The text simultaneously reserves leadership for the working class through the party and excludes defined enemies from the political community. The result is a representative-form commitment with an unresolved question about who defines eligibility, how excluded groups challenge classification, and how leadership can be replaced.

## 1.12 Leadership of allies requires victory, non-harm and education

The final section gives two necessary conditions for a leading class or party to lead allies:

1. lead them in determined struggle against a common enemy and win;
2. provide material benefit or at least not damage their interests, together with political education.

The middle-peasant example makes the rule operational: fight the landlord system with middle peasants and win; distribute part of landlord property to poorer middle peasants without damaging wealthy middle peasants; include middle-peasant activists in peasant and village governments, with a suggested one-third representation; make taxes and labor service fair; and provide political education. The same structure is extended to urban middle classes, democratic parties and people's groups.

```text
shared struggle + credible victory
+ material non-harm / benefit
+ representation
+ education
-> durable leadership of allies
```

## 1.13 Argument map

```text
overall enemy weakness + local enemy seriousness
-> calibrated strategic/local policy

policy errors vary with current state
-> change corrective direction by conditions

land reform must be led by poor peasants
+ middle peasants and other allies must not be harmed
-> coalition rather than single-class administrative capture

class/ political categories are differentiated
+ misclassification is corrected
+ property and production are protected where useful
-> transformation with preserved capacity

few killings + review + people's participation
-> bounded coercion and reduced isolation

victory + material non-harm + education
-> leadership of the united front
```

# Pass 2 — Evidence audit

See `../evidence/v4-026/evidence-ledger.md` for the itemized record. The article's strongest source evidence is its explicit operational differentiation: 25 percent income threshold, five-year and three-year behavior thresholds, consent protection for wealthy middle peasants/new rich peasants, protection of useful businesses, one-third middle-peasant representation as an example, and approval requirements for executions.

The weakest source claims are the empirical assertions that wartime cooperation with enlightened gentry was successful, that the relevant intellectual groups were mostly joinable or neutral, and that the proposed coalition would retain support. The selected text gives no complete denominators, local comparisons or independent outcome data for those claims.

The most important evidence-state separation is:

- **Source:** the document commands or asserts differentiated policy.
- **Interpretation:** differentiated classification is intended to preserve coalition and productive capacity.
- **Generalization:** high-consequence transformation should include behavior-sensitive categories, correction and non-harm boundaries.
- **Application:** any modern classifier or coercive intervention needs evidence, consent/appeal and independent review; the historical party/government committees are not automatically equivalent to those safeguards.

# Pass 3 — Reasoning reconstruction

1. **Scale separation:** strategic-level confidence and local-level caution coexist.
2. **State-conditioned correction:** the same directional error is not assumed in every state.
3. **Classifier refinement:** categories are split by behavior, income, size, origin, duration and public response.
4. **Classifier repair:** wrong composition decisions trigger correction and return of property.
5. **Coalition optimization:** leadership is treated as a function of victory, material non-harm, representation and education.
6. **Capacity preservation:** useful businesses, workers, intellectuals and some former elites are retained when they do not block the transformation.
7. **Blast-radius control:** few killings, no random killing and review seek to limit irreversible harm and political isolation.
8. **Form/operation pairing:** elected people's congresses supply a representative form while party leadership supplies the declared operating authority.

The article is not merely advocating moderation. It is designing a conditional control system that changes treatment by actor state and tries to prevent a revolutionary objective from destroying the coalition and production base needed to achieve it.

# Pass 4 — Adversarial reading

## 4.1 Strategic underestimation can become a protected identity claim

“The enemy is rotten and has no future” is an overall political forecast, not a measured force model. If leadership treats it as a protected truth, local caution may be allowed only inside an unchallengeable strategic conclusion. The article's useful scale separation does not itself create a channel for reopening the strategic layer.

## 4.2 Condition-dependent labels need observable state variables

“Winning,” “losing,” “masses seriously mobilized,” and “struggle begun” are not defined with thresholds. Different local actors can therefore classify the same situation differently and use the policy rule to justify opposite actions.

## 4.3 More categories can create more false positives

Landlord/rich peasant, bully/non-bully, large/medium/small, new/old rich peasant, enlightened/non-enlightened gentry and firm counterrevolutionary are individually plausible distinctions. Together they create a classifier stack whose errors can compound. The correction promise is incomplete without an independent counter, deadline and appeal path.

## 4.4 A 25 percent rule does not define a fair denominator

The source gives a useful numerical boundary but not the accounting rules for total income, imputed labor, seasonal variation, debt, household pooling or disputed records. A precise threshold can create an appearance of objectivity while leaving the decisive measurement political.

## 4.5 “Return as far as possible” may not repair the harm

Partial return has no deadline, valuation rule or remedy for lost income, reputation, displacement or violence. A correction operation can therefore be formally acknowledged while leaving the original cost with the misclassified person.

## 4.6 Mass-line handling is not independent adjudication

People's courts and government committees may reduce arbitrary action, but the text does not require independent judges, defense, appeal, transparent evidence or review outside the same political authority. “Only a small minority” is a claim about classifier precision, not a safeguard.

## 4.7 Productive-capacity protection is narrower than economic liberty

Protecting useful commerce and requiring continued operation after confiscation can preserve output, but it does not settle ownership, taxation, requisition, labor rights, capital exit, compensation or dispute resolution. Production can continue while rights remain insecure.

## 4.8 Conditional inclusion may preserve stigma

Using people from landlord/rich-peasant or gentry backgrounds can retain skill, but origin remains a standing suspicion. Without expiry and re-entry rules, “conditional inclusion” can become a permanent lower-trust class.

## 4.9 Representative procedures can coexist with non-contestable leadership

People's congress elections describe an institutional form; party leadership and exclusion of enemies describe a substantive power boundary. The text does not specify a final interpreter, opposition rights, leadership turnover or remedies for exclusion.

## 4.10 The endpoint cannot validate every path

The later establishment of a new state, continuation of production or survival of a coalition would not prove that every classification, execution, confiscation or local implementation was necessary or proportionate. Path harm and endpoint success must be scored separately.

# Pass 5 — Candidate mechanisms

## H1-1 — Dual-scale threat calibration

- **Source basis:** overall strategic confidence is paired with local respect for concrete enemies.
- **Textual confidence:** high.
- **Empirical confidence:** medium-low; useful as a hypothesis, not a universal law.
- **Minimum assumptions:** the strategic and local scales have different information and failure modes.
- **Expected effect:** reduces both paralysis and local overconfidence.
- **Failure conditions:** strategic confidence becomes immune to evidence; local states are not measured; leaders reward optimism.
- **Scope:** military, political and operational decisions where scale mismatch is material.

## H1-2 — State-gated corrective direction

- **Source basis:** left/right correction changes after victories, defeats, and different land-mobilization states.
- **Textual confidence:** high.
- **Empirical confidence:** unknown.
- **Minimum assumptions:** current state can be observed with enough reliability to select the corrective direction.
- **Expected effect:** prevents one fixed corrective ideology from being applied everywhere.
- **Failure conditions:** state labels are politically manipulated or lack thresholds.
- **Scope:** adaptive governance and incident response; not a permission to redefine errors after every outcome.

## H1-3 — Behavior-sensitive coalition transformation

- **Source basis:** distinguish strata and current conduct; correct misclassification; preserve middle-peasant and other allied interests.
- **Textual confidence:** high.
- **Empirical confidence:** medium-low.
- **Minimum assumptions:** behavior and effects can be observed more reliably than identity alone.
- **Expected effect:** preserves allies and reduces policy-induced opposition.
- **Failure conditions:** behavior evidence is unavailable, origin dominates, or “ally” is defined by compliance.
- **Scope:** high-consequence classification with reversible or repairable interventions.

## H1-4 — Production-preserving boundary

- **Source basis:** protect useful industry and commerce, continue useful confiscated businesses, use joint management and limit waste.
- **Textual confidence:** high.
- **Empirical confidence:** medium.
- **Minimum assumptions:** productive continuity is observable and matters to the transformation's objective.
- **Expected effect:** avoids converting political change into capacity collapse.
- **Failure conditions:** “useful” is undefined; protection excludes ownership or remedy; output hides exploitation.
- **Scope:** economic transformation and institutional change where capability continuity is critical.

## H1-5 — High-consequence action gate

- **Source basis:** few killings, no random killing, people's-court trial and government approval; criticism and struggle should avoid brutality.
- **Textual confidence:** high.
- **Empirical confidence:** low-medium; the gate's actual independence is unresolved.
- **Minimum assumptions:** review is earlier than irreversible action and can block it.
- **Expected effect:** lowers false-positive and escalation costs.
- **Failure conditions:** review is ceremonial, classifier errors are not appealable, or a numerical “few” becomes a quota.
- **Scope:** any irreversible sanction, removal, seizure or physical harm.

## H1-6 — Leadership as victory plus non-harm plus education

- **Source basis:** the final section's two conditions and the middle-peasant example.
- **Textual confidence:** high.
- **Empirical confidence:** medium-low.
- **Minimum assumptions:** allies evaluate leadership partly through outcomes and material treatment, not slogans alone.
- **Expected effect:** makes coalition leadership behaviorally testable.
- **Failure conditions:** victory is monopolized as proof, material non-harm is defined by the leader, or education replaces consent.
- **Scope:** coalition governance and alliance management.

## Open questions

1. Who owns and independently checks the income records behind the 25 percent threshold?
2. What evidence distinguishes a genuine counterrevolutionary from a mistaken or unpopular dissenter?
3. Are people's courts independent enough to reduce false positives, and what remedy exists after a wrong decision?
4. What counts as a large surplus, good conduct, success in struggle and material non-harm?
5. How were one-third middle-peasant representation and other suggested ratios implemented locally?
6. Does business protection cover ownership, taxation, requisition, labor conditions and compensation, or only continued output?
7. What later evidence confirms or revises the January policy before the next land-policy documents?

## Working judgment

The article's most reusable contribution is a conditional coalition-control architecture: scale the threat model, change corrective direction with state, separate structural transformation from indiscriminate treatment, preserve productive and informational capacity, and place irreversible actions behind review. Its central safety limitation is that the same political authority defines the categories, controls the review and leads the representative institutions. Modern transfer therefore requires independent measurement, appeal, expiry, remedy and a power-transition mechanism that the source does not provide.
