# Pass 0 — Historical frame

The selected-works editorial note presents the essay as directed against two organizational-intellectual failure modes:

1. dogmatism: treating inherited theory/text as sufficient without Chinese revolutionary experience;
2. empiricism: remaining trapped in fragmented personal experience and failing to form theory or see the whole.

This editorial framing is later retrospective context (`O7`), not automatically evidence for the author's exact 1937 intent.

The text appears after a decade of operational writings already studied in V1-001—V1-016. That sequence matters for this project: many practices later theorized here—field investigation, evidence processing, plan revision, learning from failure, and stage-sensitive knowledge—were already visible before the philosophical formulation.

# Pass 1 — Source / argument reconstruction

## 1.1 Knowledge depends on practice

The essay begins from a strong thesis:

```text
social practice
-> sensory contact with the objective world
-> knowledge begins
```

Production is treated as the most basic practical activity, while class struggle, political life, science, art and other social activities also contribute to knowledge.

The epistemological target is knowledge detached from lived historical-social processes.

## 1.2 Direct and indirect experience are not simple opposites

The text argues that an individual receives much knowledge indirectly, but such indirect knowledge ultimately originates in someone else's direct experience.

This prevents a crude reading of the essay as:

```text
only personally experienced knowledge is valid
```

Its stronger structure is:

```text
knowledge claim
-> must ultimately be connected to interaction with objective reality
```

But the reliability of transmission, abstraction and interpretation remains an additional problem.

## 1.3 Sensory knowledge is necessary but incomplete

The essay rejects the idea that direct sensation alone gives deep understanding.

```text
rich, reality-conforming sensory material
-> processing / synthesis
-> concepts, judgments, inference
-> rational knowledge
```

The famous operations—`去粗取精、去伪存真、由此及彼、由表及里`—describe transformation of evidence rather than mere accumulation.

This aligns closely with V1-013, where reconnaissance material was explicitly processed before producing judgment and plan.

## 1.4 Rational knowledge must return to practice

The argument does not stop at theory formation.

```text
practice
-> sensory knowledge
-> rational knowledge / theory
-> guided practice
```

The return to practice has two functions:

1. act on the world;
2. test and develop the theory.

Therefore theory is not complete merely because it is internally coherent.

## 1.5 Expected result is used as a validation signal

A central formulation says that knowledge is confirmed when practice reaches the result anticipated in thought; when it fails, lessons from failure can correct thought.

This is one of the most operationally important claims in the essay.

But it also needs qualification: successful outcome alone does not uniquely validate a causal explanation because multiple mechanisms can produce the same result.

## 1.6 Knowledge is historically bounded by available practice

The essay argues that one cannot concretely know the specific laws of a historical form before that form and its corresponding practice exist.

This is a strong anti-anachronism and scope principle:

```text
no relevant phenomenon / practice
-> no complete concrete knowledge of its specific laws
```

It supports the earlier corpus pattern that knowledge and strategy must carry stage and environment conditions.

## 1.7 Practical competence develops through repeated successes and failures

The essay uses war leadership and unfamiliar work as examples:

```text
initial contact / limited experience
-> repeated action
-> success + failure
-> recognition of regularities
-> improved confidence and guidance
```

This explicitly theorizes a pattern already observed in V1-004, V1-006 and V1-013.

## 1.8 Theory and experience are mutually corrective

The essay attacks both:

```text
theory without practice = dogmatism
experience without theoretical synthesis = empiricism
```

Thus its epistemology is not simply `practice over theory`.

The actual loop requires both evidence acquisition and abstraction.

## 1.9 The final model is recursive, not terminal

The closing formula is:

```text
practice
-> knowledge
-> practice
-> knowledge
-> ...
```

Each cycle is claimed to reach a higher level.

For Praxis Engine, the most important source-level fact is not the optimistic `higher level` claim but the absence of a terminal epistemic state: knowledge remains revisable through new practice.

# Pass 4 — Adversarial reading

## A1. Practice success does not uniquely establish truth

A predicted outcome may occur because:

- the causal model was correct;
- another omitted mechanism produced the result;
- luck/noise favored the action;
- measurement was biased;
- the objective was defined to match the observed result.

Therefore:

```text
success != unique causal validation
```

Practice is an essential source of evidence, but interpretation still requires competing hypotheses and controls.

## A2. Failure is also ambiguous

A good model can fail because of execution error, insufficient resources, adversarial response or random disturbance.

So:

```text
failure
-> evidence of mismatch somewhere
not necessarily
-> theory false
```

This connects to the revision-ledger distinction among model, parameter, stage, execution and environment errors.

## A3. The claim that each cycle reaches a higher level is too strong as a universal empirical statement

Real learning loops can:

- reinforce bias;
- overfit local experience;
- learn from contaminated signals;
- drift after environment change;
- become more confident while becoming less correct.

Improvement requires additional controls not fully specified here.

## A4. `Social practice is the criterion of truth` is underspecified for complex causal inference

It does not by itself specify:

- experimental design;
- counterfactual construction;
- measurement validity;
- replication;
- uncertainty quantification;
- causal identification.

Thus it should not be translated into an Agent rule as `just try it and see`.

## A5. Direct participation can improve access but also create intervention bias

The participant-observer may:

- alter the system being observed;
- become invested in prior decisions;
- selectively remember results;
- rationalize costly commitments.

Hence direct practice needs external evidence and adversarial review when stakes are high.

## A6. Political/normative commitments are intertwined with epistemological claims

The text is not a neutral modern philosophy-of-science paper. Its claims about class position and revolutionary transformation are historically and politically embedded. Transfer must separate epistemic mechanisms from ideological commitments.

# Pass 6 — Cross-article update

## What this essay genuinely strengthens

### P2 — Reality-coupled judgment and revision

Before V1-017, the corpus had already shown:

- field investigation (V1-002, V1-007);
- operational corrections (V1-004, V1-006);
- explicit reconnaissance -> judgment -> plan -> execution -> re-judgment loop (V1-013).

V1-017 provides a philosophical synthesis of that pattern. It does not create the evidence retroactively, but it increases textual confidence that the recurring operational pattern was understood as a general epistemic principle.

### P2 — Sensory/local evidence must be transformed, not merely accumulated

This is strongly aligned with V1-013's reconnaissance-processing chain and with the corpus warning against fragmented experience.

### P2 — Theory must preserve stage/context boundaries

This aligns with V1-003, V1-012, V1-015 and V1-016.

## What should NOT be promoted yet

Do not turn the final upward spiral into:

```text
agent reflection -> guaranteed improvement
```

The corpus itself already contains failure cases showing that practice can reproduce error when:

- the ontology is wrong;
- the stage is misread;
- feedback is biased;
- commitment blocks revision;
- local experience is overgeneralized.

Therefore the more defensible Praxis Engine mapping is:

```text
practice
-> evidence
-> model update
-> bounded action
-> outcome audit
-> error localization
-> revision
```

with no guarantee of monotonic improvement.
