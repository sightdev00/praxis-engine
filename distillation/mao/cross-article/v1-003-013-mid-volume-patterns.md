# V1-003 to V1-013 — Mid-Volume Cross-Context Patterns

> Scope: operational, organizational, economic and strategic texts before the famous philosophical essays.  
> Maximum confidence: `P2`. No `G2` promotion yet.

The purpose of this file is to preserve what the pre-1937 operational corpus already shows **before** reading `实践论` and `矛盾论`, so later philosophical language cannot retroactively manufacture continuity.

---

# P2-01 — Reality-sensitive strategy: state changes can invalidate previously useful action

Evidence:

- V1-003: August 1928 strategy used under a changed enemy-political state -> defeat.
- V1-004: same episode decomposed into strategic, intelligence, cohesion, support and preparation failures.
- V1-006: local defeat/encirclement cannot be generalized to whole situation; concentration/dispersal conditions change.
- V1-012: Japanese invasion changes actor relations and therefore alliance strategy.
- V1-013: laws/rules vary with time, region, nature, scale and stage.

Working claim:

```text
strategy validity = f(current state, object, level, stage, capability)
```

Boundary:
- this does not mean “anything is contextual”;
- reusable rules must preserve the conditions under which they were learned.

---

# P2-02 — Actor maps are temporal and intervention-sensitive

Evidence:

- V1-001/V1-002: social groups move as force balance and perceived success change.
- V1-004: middle actors shift in response to policy; local/kinship identities complicate class-only models.
- V1-012: external invasion reconfigures prior enemies, neutrals and potential allies.

Refinement:

An actor record needs at least:

```text
current position
interests/exposure
capability
movement triggers
cooperation value
reliability/defection risk
response to our own policy
```

Boundary:
- material interests are important but not exhaustive;
- identity, history, institutions and information can alter behavior.

---

# P2-03 — Effective capability is structured, not equal to raw quantity

Evidence:

- V1-001: concentration/structural position matters beyond population count.
- V1-002: organization transforms dispersed individuals into coordinated force.
- V1-003/V1-004: popular support, local forces, regular forces, party organization, logistics are non-equivalent capabilities.
- V1-004: membership quantity rose while resilience fell; after re-registration quantity fell while reported combat capacity increased.
- V1-013: global numerical weakness can coexist with local superiority at a decisive point.

Working claim:

```text
nominal resources
!=
usable capability
```

Audit dimensions depend on task; do not preserve the early pseudo-multiplication formula.

---

# P2-04 — Constraint topology must be inferred before prioritization

Evidence:

- V1-002 suggests a dependency/enabling-constraint structure.
- V1-003/V1-004 show system viability requiring multiple conditions simultaneously.
- V1-008 shows a hierarchical central objective supported by mutually reinforcing subsystems.
- V1-010 decomposes one subsystem into constraint-specific shortages.
- V1-013 shows strategic whole, decisive local points, and condition configurations for transition.

Current topology vocabulary:

```text
bottleneck/dependency chain
conjunctive configuration
threshold
compensatory trade-off
hierarchical objective + support system
feedback loop
unknown
```

Do not force `principal contradiction` before reconstructing the actual topology.

---

# P2-05 — Investigation/evidence should constrain strategy, but evidence requires synthesis

Evidence:

- V1-002: field investigation overturns dominant second-hand narrative.
- V1-005: social-economic investigation should determine strategy/work method; criticism needs evidence.
- V1-007: conclusion after investigation + operational investigation method.
- V1-009: abstract classes translated to observable relations for execution.
- V1-013: raw intelligence -> filtering -> relational synthesis -> judgment -> plan.

Major refinement:

```text
raw evidence
!=
conclusion
```

Evidence must be:
- sourced;
- quality-audited;
- filtered for relevance/credibility;
- related to competing mechanisms;
- combined with current self/system state.

Boundary:
- investigation ontology itself is revisable.

---

# P2-06 — Execution is part of the knowledge process

Evidence:

- V1-004: institutional/policy changes after operational failure.
- V1-006: explicit correction of earlier argument and time parameter.
- V1-007 later note: investigation presence -> investigation quality.
- V1-013: execution explicitly described as a second process of knowing; plans should change when situation differs.

Working loop:

```text
representation
-> evidence
-> model/judgment
-> commitment
-> execution
-> validation
-> revision
```

Praxis safety addition:
- action-as-learning requires reversibility/failure-cost gate.

---

# P2-07 — Objective, supporting subsystem and local method are different layers

Evidence:

- V1-005: “military only” wrongly substitutes a sub-function for whole organizational objective.
- V1-008: economic work is necessary to support current central task but cannot become the current total objective.
- V1-011: central mobilization task depends on participant life conditions and concrete methods.
- V1-013: strategic, campaign and tactical layers may use different or apparently opposite rules.

Working distinction:

```text
system objective
supporting capabilities/constraints
local tasks
implementation methods
metrics
```

Failure often occurs when one layer is mistaken for another.

---

# P2-08 — Different levels/objects can require opposite control principles

Evidence:

- V1-004: regular force concentration / local force dispersion.
- V1-006: concentration and dispersal both valid under different purposes and conditions.
- V1-010: dispersed handicraft cannot support full precision planning; concentrated key enterprises require more precise planning.
- V1-013: strategic persistence vs campaign speed; global weakness vs local superiority; defense stage vs local attack; mobility now vs greater regularity later.

Working claim:

> Never transfer a rule without preserving its object, level, operating state and objective.

---

# P2-09 — Planning resolution and horizon should match observability, capability and volatility

Evidence:

- V1-006: broad direction may be estimated while exact calendar timing was admitted wrong.
- V1-010: planning precision differs by economic organization structure.
- V1-013: highly mobile environment makes long-range concrete construction plans unrealistic; plans change frequently.

This is stronger than “be flexible.” It concerns **how much precision the environment can support**.

---

# P2-10 — Implementation method changes system response

Evidence:

- V1-008: command-style cooperative/bond mobilization can produce fragile nominal results and loss of trust.
- V1-011: Tingzhou vs Changgang/Caixi comparison links work method and participant conditions to mobilization outcomes.
- V1-005: correction mechanisms span rules, education, material conditions and information flow.

Working claim:

```text
correct objective + wrong implementation mechanism
can still fail
```

---

# P2-11 — Scale/growth metrics require stress validation

Evidence:

- V1-004: party membership growth >10k followed by collapse under white terror; smaller re-registered organization reported stronger capacity.
- V1-008: short-term formal cooperative/bond numbers can be fragile under commandism.
- V1-013: nominal global quantity and decisive local capability differ.

Candidate principle:

> evaluate systems under the conditions they are supposed to survive, not only during expansion.

Still needs later organizational/economic evidence.

---

# P2-12 — Long-term direction and current admissible action must be separated

Evidence:

- V1-006: strategic direction retained while one-year calendar forecast withdrawn.
- V1-008: wartime necessary economic work vs future peacetime projects.
- V1-010: long-term economic direction coexists with currently dominant private economy.
- V1-012: future stage transition is condition-gated rather than calendar-gated.
- V1-013: current mobility retained while preparing for future regularity.

Working claim:

```text
future target state
!=
current optimal control policy
```

# Patterns deliberately NOT promoted

## “Always find the principal bottleneck”

Rejected as universal shape after V1-003. Retained only as one possible topology.

## “Material interests are enough to model actors”

Weakened by V1-004 identity/local-history evidence.

## “First-hand investigation is always superior”

Too strong. V1-007 supports direct evidence access, but complex systems may require high-quality instruments, logs, statistics and distributed expertise.

## “More participation/democracy always improves performance”

The corpus shows value in discussion and information flow but also emphasizes coordination after commitment. No universal governance rule yet.

## “Successful outcome validates original reasoning”

Explicitly rejected by V1-006, where an earlier conclusion was retained while its reasoning was judged insufficient.
