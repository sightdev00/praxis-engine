# Variant Audit — V1-016, V2-013, V2-015, V2-016

> Status: source-critical cross-article control; not an author-method confidence upgrade

## Why this file exists

By V2-016, edition differences are no longer isolated wording changes. Across four articles, later selection/editing changes the apparent:

- causal model;
- actor model;
- classification rule;
- institutional interface;
- degree of retrospective certainty.

Because all cases pass through a related editorial tradition, they are not independent replications of a substantive Mao method. They do, however, strongly validate a research-control rule:

> When a proposition affects causality, responsibility, action authorization or later revision, the decision-time text and later selected text must remain separate evidence objects.

## Observed changes

| Article | Decision-relevant difference | What a selected-only reading risks inferring |

## Source-critical mechanism

```text
decision-time text
-> records contemporaneous causal/actor/interface model

later selected text
-> may remove tactical accommodation
-> may add later theoretical classification
-> may simplify causality

later editorial note
-> may add outcome knowledge or responsibility attribution

If merged silently:
-> hindsight becomes foresight
-> revision disappears
-> actor uncertainty looks like stable certainty
-> extracted method changes
```

## Operational rule retained

For methodologically important variants, record separately:

1. exact wording and title at each known version;
2. which causal edge, actor identity, classification boundary or action route changes;
3. what evidence was available at the earlier time;
4. whether the later change is author revision, editorial framing or unknown;
5. whether the candidate mechanism survives when only contemporaneous evidence is used.

## Confidence and boundary

- Confidence that variants materially affect our reconstruction: **high**.
- Confidence about who authored each later change: **unknown unless independently sourced**.
- This does not imply that earlier versions are automatically more truthful; they are more informative about the earlier public/decision-time model.
- Variant mirrors themselves require provenance checks against scans or published editions when a conclusion depends heavily on exact wording.

## Next falsification target

Continue checking whether later variants sometimes preserve uncertainty, weaken victorious hindsight or restore inconvenient evidence. If all audits search only for retrospective hardening, the variant process itself will acquire confirmation bias.
