# Volume II Synthesis — 毛泽东选集第二卷

> Scope: V2-001 through V2-040  
> Status: first-pass within-volume synthesis  
> Confidence ceiling: mostly `P2`; no `G3` skill rules promoted  
> Source caution: many texts belong to one 1937–1941 united-front decision chain; repeated language is often dependent evidence.

---

# 1. What changed from Volume I

Volume I emphasized investigation, base survival, organization, economic support, military reasoning and explicit philosophy. Volume II moves into larger coalition, governance and institutional problems:

```text
international actor forecasting
coalition strategy under repeated conflict
organizational learning and cadre pipelines
transitional political/economic design
public commitments and constitutional claims
security, rights and due process
crisis forecasting, remediation and after-action correction
```

The strongest Volume-II contribution is not a new slogan. It is evidence that actor models, stage forecasts and public documents must be time-versioned because they change—sometimes within weeks.

---

# 2. P2-09 — Actor models are issue-specific, time-versioned vectors

Support appears across materially different problems:

- V2-017/019/024: foreign states cannot be predicted from ideological identity alone;
- V2-023: structural class categories were revised when behavior became visible;
- V2-028: harmful effect, hidden intent and identity require different evidence;
- V2-034/037: one actor may cooperate, compete or threaten on different issues;
- V2-040: regional factions changed positions across two conflict episodes.

Working model:

```text
actor = {
  interests by issue,
  capabilities,
  observable behavior,
  reliability,
  constraints,
  current coalition role,
  update timestamp
}
```

Strong counterexamples prevent identity shortcuts:

- permanent Soviet–Chinese interest identity was contradicted;
- socialist-only aid exclusivity was contradicted by later non-socialist aid;
- one organization was repeatedly shown to contain hostile, middle and cooperative subgroups.

Still `P2`: most political cases share one wartime coalition context.

---

# 3. P2-10 — Stage labels need transition, residual and regression conditions

Volume II repeatedly rejects calendar-only stages:

- V2-009: strategic stages are directional outlines, not exact calendars;
- V2-018: stalemate can contain large campaigns and local retreat;
- V2-021: entering a stage does not guarantee maintenance; regression remains possible;
- V2-027/035: severe local incidents need not produce national transition;
- V2-038→040: a catastrophic local event was first overcalled as national rupture, then downgraded.

Required representation:

```text
stage label
-> entry evidence
-> dominant relation
-> compatible counter-events
-> maintenance conditions
-> regression/exit triggers
-> authorized / forbidden actions
```

This directly narrows “principal contradiction”: principal means highest current decision weight or transition constraint, not the only active relation. V2-040's “decides everything” is retained as a counterexample to the weaker, safer formulation.

---

# 4. P2-11 — Forecast, target, commitment and contingency are different objects

The prediction ledger was justified by repeated failures:

- V2-017: pact interpreted as durable peace shortly before invasion;
- V2-019: permanent interest identity and aid guarantee failed;
- V2-023/026: global socialist direction mixed local outcomes with universal forecasts;
- V2-034: adversary capability was stated without a horizon/scale boundary;
- V2-036: “most difficult year” lacked a metric;
- V2-038: an observed sequence prefix was overfit into a fifteen-step plan;
- V2-039/040: the nationwide-rupture forecast was downgraded.

Volume II also separates:

```text
forecast      what is expected
target        what should be caused
commitment    what an actor accepts responsibility to deliver
contingency   what must be prepared for if a branch occurs
outcome       what later happened
```

The key correction from V2-040:

> preparing for the worst branch is not assigning it probability one.

This reaches `P2` across military forecasting, political crisis and organizational targets, but final Agent rules still need non-historical transfer tests.

---

# 5. P2-12 — Competitive coalitions require a bounded conflict lifecycle

Across V2-011, 027, 028, 034–040, cooperation and resistance are not alternatives. The strongest reconstructed lifecycle is:

```text
shared objective remains
-> harmful event / restriction
-> verify trigger and attribution
-> choose minimum necessary bounded response
-> protect third parties / rights
-> stop when trigger ends
-> repair harm
-> reopen cooperation
-> monitor re-entry conditions
```

The source corpus supplies only part of this: defensive trigger, selective engagement, preparation, stop/rest and restored relations. Modern safeguards—independent fact finding, proportionality, third-party protection, appeal and escalation ceiling—are necessary additions.

The cycle is directly stress-tested by its own failure: V2-038 turns a severe event into high-certainty nationwide conspiracy, while V2-039/040 later lower the alert.

# 6. P2-13 — Organizational learning needs infrastructure and an execution bridge

Support spans different artifact types:

- V2-020: internal publication as experience exchange and organizational memory;
- V2-022: intellectual intake requires screening, integration and lifecycle management;
- V2-025: mission, collaboration and technical competence must coexist;
- V2-030: explanation, practice reports, experience synthesis, cadre training and reader feedback;
- V2-035: directive with owner, deadline, dissemination and result report.

Working loop:

```text
practice
-> report (including failure)
-> evidence/selection audit
-> synthesis
-> training
-> real task
-> outcome feedback
-> update memory and capability
```

Repeated gap: design intent is abundant; publication reach, feedback handling, skill growth and operational outcomes are rarely measured.

---

# 7. P2-14 — Formal representation and artifacts do not prove operational governance

Volume II repeatedly asks whether labels correspond to control:

- V2-028/029/031: unity/progress are decomposed into failure modes and commitments;
- V2-032: constitution, president and assembly do not alone create democratic facts;
- V2-033/036/037: three-thirds representation is promised internally and publicly;
- V2-040: regime type is nevertheless narrowly self-certified by representation plus Party leadership.

Required institutional audit:

```text
formal seats / document
vs
agenda control
information access
resource / appointment control
veto and enforcement
rights availability
appeal / replacement
observed outcomes
```

This remains a high-confidence research control, while the historical claim that the described governments were substantively democratic is unvalidated.

---

# 8. P2-15 — Trust, screening and participation should be permission-tiered

Volume II contains an instructive tension:

- V2-035: clandestine leadership roles use extremely strict screening;
- V2-037: schools, technical and construction work tolerate some infiltration risk, then update from observed work;
- V2-022: rapid intake without integration/screening creates classification and capacity risks;
- V2-033: coalition membership should not require cultural assimilation.

Modern reconstruction:

```text
role permission + blast radius + reversibility
-> evidence/screening threshold
-> initial access
-> monitored performance
-> expand / restrict / restore
```

Neither “zero suspicion” nor “open all access” is transferable. All layers require false-positive tracking, review and appeal.

---

# 9. P2-16 — High-consequence classifiers need stronger due process

The corpus itself moves toward safeguards in V2-037:

- prohibit torture;
- evidence over confession;
- avoid implicating innocents;
- narrow arrest authority;
- distinguish coerced/temporary actors;
- offer release and reintegration.

But major gaps remain:

- “anti-Communist/reactionary/hidden traitor” can be broad and unfalsifiable;
- mass hatred plus superior approval is allowed to support killing;
- peaceful opposition to the governing party is not securely protected;
- appeal and independent adjudication are absent.

The transferable rule is not a historical security policy. It is:

> the closer a classifier is to coercive action, the higher the evidence, independence, appeal and proportionality requirements.

# 10. Source criticism became a substantive result

Variant audits show later editing changes apparent methods:

- V2-013: deletes a single-cause claim and strengthens later leadership framing;
- V2-015/016/029: removes support/petition interfaces involving Chiang/government;
- V2-026: later institutional choices reweight earlier transition commitments;
- V2-031: a Central decision with branches/tasks/slogans becomes a short editorial principle;
- V2-032: genuinely corrects a factual duration while also changing rhetoric and comparison scope.

Therefore the research object includes:

```text
wording
title/date/owner
addressee/signature/channel
document role
decision-time evidence
later note/outcome knowledge
```

Variants are not always retrospective falsification; V2-032 shows real correction. This prevents the variant audit itself from becoming confirmation-biased.

# 11. Revision evidence from Volume II

High-value revisions include:

- R-012: Soviet-German pact was not durable peace;
- R-014: permanent Soviet/Chinese interest identity failed;
- R-015: actor taxonomy refined after behavior became visible;
- R-016: New Democracy text reweighted after later institutional choice;
- R-017: a concrete factual duration corrected;
- R-018: a whole earlier “left” policy bundle explicitly rejected as wrong even then;
- R-019: nationwide rupture overcall downgraded within two months.

The most important learning episode is V2-038→040:

```text
catastrophic event
-> sequence overfit / system-transition overcall
-> new constraints and event direction observed
-> calibrated downgrade
-> explicit recognition that contingency became prediction
```

The revision was real but incomplete: it did not fully revisit hidden-command attribution or upstream central communication that helped create the overcall.

---

# 12. What is not upgraded

The following remain unvalidated or contradicted:

- ideological identity predicts permanent state interest;
- crisis necessarily produces socialist revolution;
- a model case proves national transferability;
- three-thirds representation guarantees substantive pluralism;
- conflict by itself causes coalition stability;
- worst-case preparation can be credited from a non-event without opponent evidence;
- a dominant contradiction literally determines every other outcome;
- success validates all mechanisms claimed by the successful organization.

Volume II strengthens the Praxis Engine primarily by exposing these limits, not by adding universal historical laws.
