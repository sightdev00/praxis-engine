# Mao Distillation — Prediction Ledger

This ledger tracks **prospective, outcome-bearing claims** before later historical knowledge silently converts them into narratives of foresight.

Purpose:

```text
prediction at time t
-> observable outcome definition
-> later evidence
-> confirmed / partial / contradicted / unidentifiable
-> what changed in the model
```

## Rules

1. Prefer predictions made before the relevant outcome.
2. Record the exact time horizon, threshold and observable consequence when stated.
3. Do not mark a prediction confirmed merely because later history is broadly compatible with it.
4. Separate:
   - direction forecast;
   - timing forecast;
   - threshold/capability forecast;
   - actor-behavior forecast;
   - causal forecast.
5. A prediction can be directionally right but temporally/causally wrong.
6. Later editorial notes are `O7` evidence; use them as later evidence, not as original support.
7. Record unidentifiable cases rather than force binary success/failure.

## PRED-001 — One-year timetable for winning Jiangxi

- Source/date: V1-006 `星星之火，可以燎原`, 1930-01-05, reflecting an earlier proposal.
- Type: timing forecast.
- Prediction: an earlier plan had proposed approximately one year to obtain Jiangxi.
- Outcome evidence: V1-006 explicitly states that attaching a one-year period was wrong.
- Status: **contradicted (timing)**.
- Important nuance: the broader direction judgment was retained while the calendar parameter was withdrawn.
- Method lesson: evaluate direction and timing parameters separately.

## PRED-002 — Humane-prisoner policy will later affect Japanese soldiers

- Source/date: V2-005 `和英国记者贝特兰的谈话`, 1937-10-25.
- Type: causal/behavioral forecast.
- Prediction: policy of humane treatment and release of prisoners, although effect is currently limited, will have future effect on enemy-soldier disintegration/sympathy.
- Observable outcome needed: later evidence that exposed soldiers' attitudes, defection, cooperation or information behavior changed because of the policy rather than unrelated causes.
- Status: **unresolved**.
- Audit risk: later wartime propaganda may over-attribute enemy disintegration to this policy.

## PRED-003 — KMT political/organizational reform is possible

- Source/date: V2-006 `上海太原失陷以后抗日战争的形势和任务`, 1937-11-12.
- Type: actor/institutional forecast.
- Prediction: KMT political/organizational reform is both necessary and possible under pressures from Japan, united-front policy, public demand and internal new forces.
- Later evidence: Selected Works note [5] explicitly says the broad public/CCP hope that KMT would undertake democratic reform was not realized, citing later retrospective judgment.
- Status: **contradicted / overestimated reform probability**.
- Caveat: some limited wartime adjustments occurred; the prediction concerns the substantive reform envisioned in the article.
- Method lesson: external pressure may create incentives for reform without overcoming incumbent power/institutional interests.

## PRED-004 — 250k–300k new-type troops within 6–12 months will show battlefield turning signs

- Source/date: V2-006, 1937-11-12.
- Type: threshold + timing + causal forecast.
- Prediction: if 250,000–300,000 troops with a new political spirit are established within six months to one year, the battlefield will begin to show a turn and this will form the military basis for strategic counteroffensive.
- Observable outcome needed:
  1. whether capability threshold was actually reached in time;
  2. whether a measurable turning point followed;
  3. whether the troop transformation caused the turn rather than other variables.
- Status: **unresolved / identification required**.
- Audit priority: high, because the claim is unusually specific.

## PRED-005 — Anti-surrender factors will remain stronger than national surrender forces

- Source/date: V2-006, 1937-11-12.
- Type: scenario/actor-force forecast.
- Prediction: factors preventing surrender currently dominate and can prevent surrenderism from taking national power, though splitting/coexistence risk remains.
- Observable outcome: whether national resistance persists; whether large collaboration/splitting occurs; whether the proposed causal factors are the reason.
- Status: **partially testable later; unresolved now**.
- Important nuance: coexistence/splitting is itself one of the article's branches, so later collaboration does not automatically falsify the broader forecast.
