# Mao Distillation — Prediction Ledger

This ledger tracks **prospective, outcome-bearing claims** before later historical knowledge silently converts them into narratives of foresight.

Purpose:

```text
prediction at time t
-> observable outcome definition
-> later evidence
-> confirmed / partial / contradicted / unidentifiable
-> what changed in the model
```

## Rules

1. Prefer predictions made before the relevant outcome.
2. Record the exact time horizon, threshold and observable consequence when stated.
3. Do not mark a prediction confirmed merely because later history is broadly compatible with it.
4. Separate:
   - direction forecast;
   - timing forecast;
   - threshold/capability forecast;
   - actor-behavior forecast;
   - causal forecast.
5. A prediction can be directionally right but temporally/causally wrong.
6. Later editorial notes are `O7` evidence; use them as later evidence, not as original support.
7. Record unidentifiable cases rather than force binary success/failure.
8. For dense forecasting texts, split claims by actor, horizon, precondition, outcome and causal explanation rather than grading a whole paragraph at once.

## PRED-001 — One-year timetable for winning Jiangxi

- Source/date: V1-006 `星星之火，可以燎原`, 1930-01-05, reflecting an earlier proposal.
- Type: timing forecast.
- Prediction: an earlier plan had proposed approximately one year to obtain Jiangxi.
- Outcome evidence: V1-006 explicitly states that attaching a one-year period was wrong.
- Status: **contradicted (timing)**.
- Important nuance: the broader direction judgment was retained while the calendar parameter was withdrawn.
- Method lesson: evaluate direction and timing parameters separately.

## PRED-002 — Humane-prisoner policy will later affect Japanese soldiers

- Source/date: V2-005 `和英国记者贝特兰的谈话`, 1937-10-25.
- Type: causal/behavioral forecast.
- Prediction: policy of humane treatment and release of prisoners, although effect is currently limited, will have future effect on enemy-soldier disintegration/sympathy.
- Observable outcome needed: later evidence that exposed soldiers' attitudes, defection, cooperation or information behavior changed because of the policy rather than unrelated causes.
- Status: **unresolved**.
- Audit risk: later wartime propaganda may over-attribute enemy disintegration to this policy.

## PRED-003 — KMT political/organizational reform is possible

- Source/date: V2-006 `上海太原失陷以后抗日战争的形势和任务`, 1937-11-12.
- Type: actor/institutional forecast.
- Prediction: KMT political/organizational reform is both necessary and possible under pressures from Japan, united-front policy, public demand and internal new forces.
- Later evidence: Selected Works note [5] explicitly says the broad public/CCP hope that KMT would undertake democratic reform was not realized, citing later retrospective judgment.
- Status: **contradicted / overestimated reform probability**.
- Caveat: some limited wartime adjustments occurred; the prediction concerns the substantive reform envisioned in the article.
- Method lesson: external pressure may create incentives for reform without overcoming incumbent power/institutional interests.

## PRED-004 — 250k–300k new-type troops within 6–12 months will show battlefield turning signs

- Source/date: V2-006, 1937-11-12.
- Type: threshold + timing + causal forecast.
- Prediction: if 250,000–300,000 troops with a new political spirit are established within six months to one year, the battlefield will begin to show a turn and this will form the military basis for strategic counteroffensive.
- Observable outcome needed:
  1. whether capability threshold was actually reached in time;
  2. whether a measurable turning point followed;
  3. whether the troop transformation caused the turn rather than other variables.
- Status: **unresolved / identification required**.
- Audit priority: high, because the claim is unusually specific.

## PRED-005 — Anti-surrender factors will remain stronger than national surrender forces

- Source/date: V2-006, 1937-11-12.
- Type: scenario/actor-force forecast.
- Prediction: factors preventing surrender currently dominate and can prevent surrenderism from taking national power, though splitting/coexistence risk remains.
- Observable outcome: whether national resistance persists; whether large collaboration/splitting occurs; whether the proposed causal factors are the reason.
- Status: **partially testable later; unresolved now**.
- Important nuance: coexistence/splitting is itself one of the article's branches, so later collaboration does not automatically falsify the broader forecast.

## PRED-006 — European war will shift to direct Anglo-French vs German-Italian conflict

- Source/date: V2-017 `关于国际新形势对新华日报记者的谈话`, 1939-09-01.
- Type: near-term direction forecast.
- Prediction: the prior one-sided/non-intervention pattern in Europe will give way to direct major war between the rival imperialist blocs.
- Preconditions: German expansion, failure of Anglo-French-Soviet negotiations, current alliance structure.
- Time horizon: described as imminent / new stage.
- Observable outcome: formal and sustained direct war among the named major powers.
- Status: **requires date-sensitive audit**. Germany invaded Poland on 1939-09-01 and Britain/France declared war on 1939-09-03; the exact timing of interview/publication relative to the invasion matters for judging predictive content.
- Alternative explanation: a very-short-horizon inference from already visible mobilization is weaker evidence of deep foresight than a long-range forecast.

## PRED-007 — United States will remain outside the war temporarily

- Source/date: V2-017, 1939-09-01.
- Type: actor-behavior + timing forecast.
- Prediction: the United States will temporarily avoid entering the war, under neutrality, with an inferred later aim of shaping postwar leadership.
- Observable outcome: formal belligerent status over the following period; separate from inferred motive.
- Later broad fact: the U.S. did not enter the war formally until December 1941.
- Status: **directionally confirmed for the explicitly temporary non-entry claim; motive unidentifiable from this fact alone**.
- Method lesson: separate observable behavior from inferred strategic intent.

## PRED-008 — Japan will reduce large frontal strategic offensives while increasing political/economic pressure

- Source/date: V2-017, 1939-09-01.
- Type: operating-mode forecast.
- Prediction: Japan's objective toward China remains, but large-scale frontal offensives become less likely while political `以华制华`, economic `以战养战`, and occupied-area sweeps intensify.
- Observable outcome: frequency/scale of major frontal operations vs occupied-area operations, political collaboration policy and economic extraction after September 1939.
- Status: **unresolved / requires military-history comparison**.
- Important nuance: even if the mode forecast is right, the claim that the underlying objective was permanently fixed is a separate hypothesis.

## PRED-009 — Strategic stalemate has arrived, but strategic counteroffensive has not

- Source/date: V2-017, 1939-09-01; clarified in V2-018, 1939-09-16.
- Type: stage/state forecast.
- Prediction: strategic retreat has basically ended under stated conditions; the next state is stalemate/preparation, not immediate strategic counteroffensive. Local offensives/retreats remain possible.
- Observable outcome: strategic front/territory dynamics, relative force development, task composition and whether the proposed preparation period is distinguishable from prior retreat.
- Status: **unresolved as a model classification**.
- Audit risk: if stage definitions can absorb any local counterexample, the model becomes weakly falsifiable.

## PRED-010 — Soviet support and Chinese national-liberation interests will remain permanently aligned

- Source/date: V2-019 `苏联利益和人类利益的一致`, 1939-09-28.
- Type: actor-reliability / long-horizon forecast.
- Prediction: Soviet interests and China's national-liberation interests will not conflict and will remain permanently consistent; even a Soviet-Japanese non-aggression agreement would not limit Soviet aid to China.
- Preconditions asserted by source: Soviet state identity and stated policy principles.
- Observable outcome: later Soviet-Japanese agreements, aid levels, diplomatic priorities and cases in which Soviet security interests conflict with Chinese interests.
- Status: **unresolved in corpus sequence; high-priority adversarial audit**.
- Current evidence warning: the same article's Poland model is materially weakened by later evidence of hidden Soviet-German territorial arrangements, so identity-based reliability claims should receive reduced prior confidence.
